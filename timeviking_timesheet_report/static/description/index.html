Free timesheet report in PDF and Excel

Pick a period and one or more employees, then print a clean PDF in your company document layout or download an Excel file. Works on Odoo Community and Enterprise with only the Timesheets app.

The wizard

Timesheets > Reporting > TimeViking Time Report, or Print on an employee.

PDF

One block per employee with date, project, task, description and time, a total per employee and overall.

Excel

Real dates and durations (h:mm) that add up, ready for filtering and pivots.

Features

  • Any period, several employees at once (empty = all)
  • PDF in your own company layout: logo, colours, address, footer
  • Excel (.xlsx) with real dates and h:mm durations
  • Totals per employee and overall
  • Access rights respected: users print their own time, approvers everyone's
  • Multi-company; English, Dutch, French, German, Spanish and Italian
  • A small "Powered by TimeViking" line, which you can switch off in Settings > Timesheets
